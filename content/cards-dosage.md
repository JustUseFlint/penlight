@deck dosage | Dosage Calculation Cheat Sheet | Dosage Calculation
@topic Dosage Calculation
@tag DM1
1 g => 1,000 mg.
1 mg => 1,000 mcg.
1 tsp => 5 mL.
1 tbsp => 15 mL.
1 kg => 2.2 lb.
Leading zero rule => Write 0.5 mg, never .5 mg.
Trailing zero rule => Write 5 mg, never 5.0 mg.
@tag DM2
Desired over have formula => Desired dose ÷ available strength × quantity = amount to give.
@tag DM3
Convert pounds to kg => Divide by 2.2.
mg/kg dosing => Dose per kg × weight in kg.
@tag DM4
Pump rate => Total volume (mL) ÷ time (hours) = mL/hr.
Drip rate => Volume (mL) × drop factor (gtt/mL) ÷ time (minutes) = gtt/min.
Microdrip set => 60 gtt/mL, so gtt/min equals mL/hr.
@tag DM5
Units/hr to mL/hr => Ordered units/hr ÷ concentration (units/mL).
@tag DM6
Safe dose range check => Calculate low and high ends (dose/kg × weight) and compare with the order.
